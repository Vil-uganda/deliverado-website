How It Works

One order. Every step connected.

The product should make the journey visible from the first order to confirmed delivery.

01

Customer orders

Browse restaurants and place an order in minutes.

02

Restaurant receives order

The kitchen is notified instantly with every detail.

03

Restaurant confirms

The restaurant accepts and starts preparing the food.

04

Rider receives delivery

A nearby rider is assigned to the order.

05

Rider picks up

The rider collects the order from the restaurant.

06

Customer tracks delivery

Follow the rider from pickup to your door.

07

Delivery completed

Proof of delivery confirms the order arrived.

Home/About
About

We're building the infrastructure behind movement.

Deliverado began with a simple idea: moving something across Uganda should be easier. We are starting with food delivery, then building toward the network businesses, online sellers, customers and riders can rely on.

Built for Uganda

A delivery network designed around local movement.

Deliverado is being built around customers, restaurants, merchants and independent riders operating in the Ugandan market.

Local Businesses

Built for restaurants and merchants.

A delivery network that supports how local commerce already moves.

Mobile-first

Designed for everyday digital commerce.

A simple experience shaped around the device already in your hand.

Delivery Visibility

Know what is happening.

Follow the journey from pickup through confirmed delivery.

Flexible Network

Designed to expand as density grows.

Start with focused routes, then add coverage as the network strengthens.

Made for how Uganda moves and pays.

Pay the easy way with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. And find any address without street names: search a nearby landmark, drop a pin, or add a plot number so your rider finds you the first time.

We are onboarding restaurants, building the rider network, preparing the technology and listening to customers before opening the platform.
